**Third-party data** remains under the terms of its original provider (Eurostat, ELSTAT, UN DESA, HMD, HFD and others). The terms, required credit line and whether commercial reuse is allowed are recorded for each source in the `licence` field of `pipeline/src/grpop/sources/registry.yaml`. Derived datasets are published under CC BY 4.0 only where the source terms allow it, and each release states the source terms that apply.

Known exceptions:

- **Map boundaries.** NUTS boundaries come from Eurostat/GISCO and are licensed for non-commercial use only. Every map shows "© EuroGeographics for the administrative boundaries". The boundary geometry is not covered by CC BY 4.0; the statistical values shown on the map are.
- **ELSTAT data.** When published values are modified or translated from ELSTAT data, the release says so and states that ELSTAT bears no responsibility for the result, as ELSTAT's reuse policy requires.
- **Eurostat data for non-EU/EFTA countries** is for non-commercial reuse only.
- **HMD and HFD input data** are not redistributed. Only HMD/HFD estimates are used, with their required citation.
